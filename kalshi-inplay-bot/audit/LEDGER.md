# LEDGER — every claim, and what actually backs it

Read-only audit, 2026-07-30. One row per distinct claim.

**Chat exports were unavailable** (`audit/pro_chats.json` / `audit/max_chats.json` do not
exist on this machine — confirmed by a full-user-tree search). Every row below is
reconstructed from project files, results files, logs and git history. Claims that could
only have come from a chat are marked `PENDING (chat export)`.

Status key — applied strictly:

- **SETTLED** — reproducible artifact, adequate n at the correct unit, out-of-sample or
  structural/arithmetic. Safe to build on.
- **SUGGESTIVE** — real artifact, but underpowered, unclustered, in-sample or uncorrected.
- **UNVERIFIED** — asserted with no locatable artifact.
- **BROKEN** — artifact exists but has an invalidating defect.
- **RETRACTED** — corrected later. **⚠ marks a retraction that is still stated as fact
  somewhere on disk.**

Projects: **P1** `kalshi` (live tennis bot + backtest) · **P2** `kalshi markets`
(exchange-wide scan) · **P3** `tennis copy trade` (Polymarket tennis) ·
**P4** PTIS (`Codex\2026-07-23\…-master-2`) · **P5** `weather-market-bot` ·
**P6** Discord trading server.

## What this ledger says in one paragraph

Across six threads and 124 distinct claims, **not one tradeable edge is SETTLED anywhere.**
What *is* settled is a long list of well-executed negative results — Kalshi tennis momentum
loses ~9¢/trade against a ~4¢ cost base; Polymarket tennis wallet skill returns zero BH
discoveries across 42,652 wallets; informed flow exists but dies inside 15 seconds against a
~66-second visibility delay; weather models lose to the market ask; leaderboard consensus
copying loses money; no-arb violations are real and sub-contract-sized; Kalshi's trade feed
carries no account identifier at all.

The claims that would change a trading decision if true are precisely the ones with **no
locatable artifact**: the 0.60–0.95 price band (C042), the Betfair correlation (C009), the
live bot's entry-price and stop-width gates (C011, C012), the maker-fill result (C005b), the

Three structural observations about the corpus as a whole:
