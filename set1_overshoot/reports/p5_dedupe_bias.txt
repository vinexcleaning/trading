half the time. Anything else means the rule is reading the answer.

Winning side's share of the event's total volume: mean 0.5250, median 0.5152
Winning side has the larger volume in 53.6% of events.

MECHANISM. Kalshi runs a separate order book for each side of a match, so
the two books do not share volume. As a match resolves, trading concentrates
in the side that is winning. Reading volume after settlement therefore
reveals the outcome, and picking the busier book picks the winner 53.6% of
the time.

WHY IT MATTERED SO MUCH. The analysis orients every match to the favourite, so
it splits on whether the kept (busier) player IS the favourite. When it is, the
winner-biased side is the favourite and the favourite appears to overperform its
price. When it is not, the same bias makes the favourite appear to
underperform. One selection error, entering the two halves with opposite sign.

FIX: lexicographic ticker order, 0.4969 (z = -0.88). Player-name
abbreviations cannot know who wins. Measured, not assumed.
