**Gate lifted for this test only.** Justification in `PREREGISTRATION_PARTB.md` §5c:
the Phase 3 gate exists to stop slicing a *null*, and θ = −2.42 pp (p = 0.0009) is
not null. Buckets were fixed before running.

**Power, computed in advance.** Per-contract sd ≈ 45 ¢, so detecting a +2 ¢ edge at
80% power needs **n ≈ 3,970**. The whole event sample is **3,436**. No bucket can
be individually powered for a 2 ¢ edge. Each cell reports its own MDE; a cell whose
apparent edge is smaller than its MDE is marked UNTESTABLE.

`effect` = 100×(observed − implied). `bar` = 100×(breakeven − implied), **computed
per bucket**. `net` = effect − bar, exactly, and equals ¢/contract.
