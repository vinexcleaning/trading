======================================================================================
READING THIS
======================================================================================
The positive control must show a clearly non-zero r -- match win rate
is a real, stable skill. If a trait's r is not meaningfully above its
null, there are no stable individual differences to model: the
variation between players is what binomial noise produces on its own,
and the feature should be dropped rather than shrunk.
