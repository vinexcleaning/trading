SHARP BOOK vs KALSHI PRE-MATCH PRICE
============================================================================================
Kalshi quote anchored 6h before `occurrence_datetime`.
That field is at or after the match END for many markets; anchoring
on it directly leaks the result (4.1% of quotes sat outside 2c-98c
and were 100% correct). At -6h that signature is gone.

NOTE: tennis-data.co.uk carries Pinnacle for only 5.1% of 2026 rows --
they stopped publishing it. The sharp benchmark used here is the
BETFAIR EXCHANGE closing price (93.6% coverage in 2026), which for
tennis is at least as sharp as Pinnacle. Book average is the control.

No model, nothing fitted, no parameters tuned -> no holdout required.
Every figure below reports its own n. CIs are 4,000-sample bootstraps.
