## ⚠ The convergence with what he told us, which nobody planned

He said **college football is more predictable than the NFL** and **basketball
is predictable because it is high-scoring**. The factory measured that
independently and confirmed the college-football call (37c against the NFL's
11c) and found college basketball the most confidently priced sport of twelve.

**The cheapest deep markets on this whole exchange are college football
futures, college basketball, and NFL season-long markets.** His instinct and
the cost measurement point at the same shelf, and it was reached from two
directions that did not know about each other.

**That is a lead, not a finding.** "The market prices it confidently" and "we
can beat that price" are different sentences and may point opposite ways —
`BROAD-01.md` §3 says so and it is right.

## What this list is NOT

- **Not screened for whether an edge exists.** Cost to enter and size at the
  offer only. Every one of these could still be perfectly priced.
- **Not screened for whether the data exists** to form a view.
- **Season-long markets settle slowly.** Money is tied up for months, which is
  a real cost this list does not price.
- **"Ever named by a strategy here" is read off `specs/`**, so a family
  examined and never written up counts as untouched.
