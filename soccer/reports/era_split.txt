HAS THE COMEBACK RATE CHANGED OVER TEN YEARS?
==============================================================================

Why it matters: every Kalshi price in this folder is from 2026,
and the comeback rates are an average of 2015-2024. If the game
has changed, that average is the wrong thing to price against.

Five substitutes became permanent in 2022, which is the specific
reason to look rather than a general worry.

    2015-2018:  21370 matches
    2019-2021:  16680 matches
    2022-2024:  18877 matches

Out of 100 matches in that state, how often the team behind
came back and won. Bigger is worse for betting against them.
