DOES THE NEAR-CERTAINTY GAP EXIST IN OTHER SPORTS?
==============================================================================

Soccer found that Kalshi stops quoting an outcome once it
becomes near-certain, so the bet 'buy the thing that is 97% to
happen' is absent rather than badly priced.

This asks the same question of every sport Kalshi runs per-game,
using only the price -- no scores, no clocks, no sport knowledge.

  near-certain = somebody bidding 95 cents or more
  middling     = somebody bidding 40 to 70 cents
  buyable      = there is an offer below 100 you could hit

**The comparison between the two columns is the result.** A
thin book would show few offers everywhere and would mean
nothing about near-certainty.
