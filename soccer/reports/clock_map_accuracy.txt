COMPARE: SO009 measured `kickoff + displayed minute` at a
median of 17.52 minutes out, on 362 events. That method walks
through halftime and through every minute of stoppage. This
one never leaves the half it is in.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT SAY. It measures how well the map places a
minute ESPN also timestamped. Minutes with no nearby anchor
are refused rather than guessed, so the errors above are the
errors of the readings actually taken -- but a match with
sparse anchors contributes fewer readings, not worse ones.
