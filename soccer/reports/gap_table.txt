THE GAP: WHAT HAPPENS, WHAT KALSHI CHARGES, AND THE DIFFERENCE
==============================================================================

Prices: 10878 minute-readings from 206 matches inside Kalshi's
69-day window, read at every displayed minute whether or not
anything had just happened.

Rates: 18456 matches, 2022-2024 only. Not the ten-year
average -- comebacks late in a one-goal game became more common
after 2022 and the ten-year number would understate them.

**These are two different sets of matches.** A fair price built
from the rates is a hypothesis about 2026, not a measurement.

4. THE OBJECTION THAT COULD HAVE FAKED ALL OF THIS
==============================================================================

Sections 2 and 3 compare a rate averaged over 23 competitions
against prices that came from whichever were in season. They
are not the same competitions:

    fifa.world                 3145 readings   28.9%
    fifa.friendly              2782 readings   25.6%
    usa.nwsl                   1385 readings   12.7%
    mex.1                      1141 readings   10.5%
    chi.1                       927 readings    8.5%
    usa.1                       905 readings    8.3%
    col.1                       593 readings    5.5%

**More than half is the World Cup and international friendlies,
and there is no European league in it at all.** A friendly is
barely the same sport -- six substitutions and nobody trying --
so an average that includes ordinary league football could be
flattering or damning here purely by mixture.

So: every reading is re-compared against ITS OWN competition's
rate at that exact minute and scoreline, 2022-2024. No averaging
across competitions happens at all.
