==============================================================================
5. WHAT THE NUMBER HAS TO BEAT
==============================================================================

Computed from this repo's one fee implementation, held to
settlement. Bigger is better for you -- it means you can afford
more comebacks before the bet loses money.

  you pay                      if you did it 100 times   comebacks allowed per 100
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 90 cents           win 10 cents 91x, lose 90 cents 9x                        9.37
 93 cents            win 7 cents 93x, lose 93 cents 7x                        6.54
 95 cents            win 5 cents 95x, lose 95 cents 5x                        4.67
 96 cents            win 4 cents 96x, lose 96 cents 4x                        3.73
 97 cents            win 3 cents 97x, lose 97 cents 3x                        2.80
 98 cents            win 2 cents 98x, lose 98 cents 2x                        1.86

The price is not in this table yet. Kalshi keeps about 69 days
of market history, so a real price can only be attached to very
recent matches -- that is a separate job on a much smaller set.
