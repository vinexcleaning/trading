THE VERDICT, BY THE RULE FIXED BEFORE LOOKING
==============================================================================

The pre-registration requires **216 matches** before a
verdict is allowed - the number needed to see a 1-cent effect
against a per-match spread of 7.35 cents.

**73 matches. That is below 216, so the verdict is CANNOT TELL** - and it was pre-registered as
the expected outcome, not chosen after seeing the number.

The idea is neither alive nor dead. It is unmeasured.
Reading the result above as evidence either way is exactly
the mistake this folder documented four times.
