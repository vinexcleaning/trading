## Livedesk — the one baseball window

**As of 2026-08-12.**

**It is built and it opens.** Double-click `livedesk\run.bat`. One card: which
team, why in plain English, the price, how much goes in, what you make if it
wins and lose if it loses, and how many times out of 100 it has to win just to
break even. 46 checks pass.

**The button copies the bet and opens that one game's Kalshi page. You place
it.** There is no key in that folder and no order-sending code, so nothing can
fire while you are asleep. The window comes to you — it jumps to the front and
chimes when a bet qualifies, so you never go browsing Kalshi.

**Your four corrections are all in, and you were right on every one.** One bet
per SIGNAL, not per game, so a genuinely different reason on the same game is
allowed — two per game at most, and never on top of a bet that is losing. The
cut-off is now $50 hard floor plus a 35% drop from its best, so at $300 it
would allow a $105 loss, not $30. And the profit figure is checked against your
real balance: **type what Kalshi shows into the box, and if the two are more
than a dollar apart the window shows no profit at all and stops offering bets.**

**On the $32 the tennis app got wrong — I found it.** When a market you WON
settles, it drops off the list the app was adding up, so the total fell exactly
when you won. There is a second one still in that app: a game that has finished
but not paid out yet is counted as worth nothing. I could not run it to prove
this, only read the code, so treat it as likely rather than certain — which is
why the new check catches the disagreement whether or not I am right.

**One thing to know.** It already caught a bet going stale: the baseball chat
fixed a fault yesterday that cut one game's value from 99 cents to 71, and the
window dropped that bet and said so in the log.

**Nothing needs deciding right now.** Open it, type your balance in once, and
place one bet when you are ready.
