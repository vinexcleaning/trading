## Tennis — the heavy-favourite question is closed

**The question was: the market slightly underprices heavy favourites, but buying
them at the offer loses money. Does waiting to be sold to instead fix it?**

**Answer: no, and it is close enough to be worth explaining.**

The market really is wrong about favourites priced around 90 to 95 cents — they
win about 2 more times in 100 than the price says. **But crossing the spread
costs more than that.** Waiting instead recovers about a penny in every dollar
— **but only if you are first in the queue.** If you are behind other people's
orders, you are back to losing slightly.

**Best case +1.75 dollars per 100 risked; realistic case −0.28.** And the best
case sits exactly on the edge of what pure luck produces, so it is not evidence
of anything yet.

**Two things I got wrong and corrected.**

I told you earlier that you only ever get filled when the favourite is in
trouble, by 4 to 8 points. **On the full data that is true for the 75 to 90 cent
range and not for the 90 to 95 range, where it is about 1 point.** The earlier
number came from a half-downloaded file.

And **my safety check failed and caught a mistake of mine.** I ran the deliberate
wrong version — quoting the opposite side, which should lose badly — and it came
out *better* than the real one. That is impossible, so I stopped and found it: I
had swapped which side filled me but forgotten to swap the profit calculation.
Fixed, the wrong version now loses 3.58 where the right version gains 1.75, which
is what it should do.

**One thing worth knowing for the parlay idea specifically.** The effect
**reverses above 95 cents** — the very heaviest favourites are genuinely
*over*priced. That is exactly where a parlay would want to live, so there is
nothing there to build on.

**WHAT I NEED FROM YOU — and it is the second time this exact question has
decided a study.** When you have left an order sitting on Kalshi instead of
taking the price, **did it actually get filled, and roughly how big were you?**
Front of the queue makes this bet worth +1.75 per 100; back of the queue makes it
−0.28. Kalshi does not publish where you sit, and no amount of downloading will
tell me. **Your own experience settles it, and it would settle two studies at
once.**
