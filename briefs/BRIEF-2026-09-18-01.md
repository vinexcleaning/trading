## De-vig: the parlay tape is saved, and the 9-hour outage is fixed

**As of 2026-09-15.** All thirty-two coordinator messages answered.

**The parlay data is captured and it was a race worth running. 277,861 multi-leg bets and 2.2 million individual legs**, going back to 9 August — **anything that finished before that is already deleted and gone forever.** It now refreshes every six hours and restarts itself if the machine reboots.

**⚠ And I nearly saved 6 of every 100 of it.** My first run reported exactly 8,000 for three different categories. That is not a number the exchange gave me — **it is my own page limit**, and it looked entirely believable. Removing it turned 8,000 into **142,400** for the biggest category alone. **This is the third time in this folder a self-imposed limit has been mistaken for a measurement.** A count that exactly equals your own cap is never a measurement.

**The nine-hour hole in our recording is fixed, but not the way I was told to fix it.** I was asked to add a missing timeout to the bookmaker connection. **There was no missing timeout.** The real cause: a timeout in this software limits each individual attempt, **not the total** — and with five retries across 328 requests, the thing can legally take **fifteen hours while never once "timing out"**.

**The more important half: our restart system cannot see this kind of failure at all.** It restarts anything that has *stopped*. That process never stopped — it was busy for nine hours — so the watchdog looked at it every ten minutes and correctly reported it healthy. **It checks whether something is alive; the failure was that it stopped making progress.** I fixed it by making the recorder give up on itself and quit after 30 minutes, which the existing watchdog then treats as a normal restart. I did not touch the shared watchdog, which is deliberately built so it can never kill anything.

**One thing I was warned about and would have got wrong:** 93 out of every 100 parlays show no price at all, because they are only priced when somebody asks for a quote. Every other recorder we have sensibly throws those rows away. **Doing that here would have saved 7% of the data and called it finished.**

**Needs from you: nothing. Continuing.**
