CLAIM 1 — lead-lag ETH->XRP +0.1544
====================================================================================================
  artifact present: n_bars=1814399
    best lead 1s = +0.1544  SE=0.00129  -> 120 SE

  Does the independence finding invalidate this?
  NO. That finding says the four assets' SETTLEMENT SIGNS are
  correlated. The lead-lag test MEASURES cross-asset
  correlation — it is the object of study, not a confound.

  But the CI deserves scrutiny: SE was taken as 1/sqrt(n),
  which assumes independent observations. 1-second returns are
  mildly autocorrelated, so the true SE is larger.
  Even a 10x understated SE leaves the effect at ~4 SE.

  The number that actually decided it was ECONOMIC, not
  statistical: 0.38c of edge against a 1.00c tick, needing a
  correlation of 0.575-1.113 vs 0.1544 observed. Widening the
  CI does not move a sub-tick signal above one tick.
