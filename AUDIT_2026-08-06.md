# AUDIT — every test ever run, across every market

**2026-08-06.** Part 1 of a two-part pass. **Nothing was fixed and nothing was
re-run.** This file lists defects, ranked by whether they could flip a verdict.
The readable deliverable is [SCOREBOARD.md](SCOREBOARD.md).

Sources read: [LEDGER.md](LEDGER.md) (261 rows), [GUARDS.md](GUARDS.md) (22
guards + coverage matrix), and the results/handoff documents of every project
that ever ran a market test — `set1_overshoot`, `crypto`, `wallet-copy-study`,
`kalshi-tennis`, `kalshi-market-scan`, `kalshi-inplay-bot`, `bot-forensics`,
`bot-hunt`, `market-selection`, `soccer`, `polymarket-tennis-copy`,
`ptis-polymarket`, `mlb`, plus `kalshi-chat-audit`'s 129 archived claims.

## 0. The headline finding of the audit itself

**The programme's self-correction machinery works.** 45 recorded retractions,
every one shrinking the edge; 22 reusable guards; a repo-wide test that makes it
impossible to reimplement the fee formula again. Leaks were caught by the
canaries designed to catch them (T010/T011, bot-hunt Amendment A1, B025), and in
three separate cases a project caught its **own** bug before publishing.

**So the defects below are not "the results are wrong."** With two exceptions
(D1, D3) they are *unfinished verifications*, *stale rows*, and *unledgered
projects* — and the repo's own history says the third category is the dangerous
one:

> **Ledgering a previously-unledgered project has produced a verdict-relevant
> defect on 2 out of 2 attempts.** Section 6 (`kalshi-market-scan`) surfaced
> K015 = W011 — the same dead number carrying two different statuses in two
> projects. Section 7 (`bot-forensics`) surfaced B005a and corrected two stale
> rows that had been wrong for months. **Four projects are still unledgered.**

## 1. DEFECTS THAT COULD FLIP A VERDICT

### D1 · `set1_overshoot` S022 and S023 were computed on the VOID event set and never re-run
**Verdict at risk:** *"Tennis set-1: no edge in either direction."*
**Ledger status:** both **BROKEN**, unfixed.

S011's dedupe bug (kept the higher-`volume_fp` side; P(kept wins) = 0.5356,
z = +10.0) voided the event universe. S001–S006 were re-run on the clean
universe. **S022 (retirement add-back, −0.004¢) and S023 (the fade side loses in
all 6 configurations) were not.** S023 is *the other half of the verdict* — the
claim that you cannot profit by trading the undershoot in the opposite
direction. The ledger's own note says the cost arithmetic "likely carries the
conclusion", which is an expectation, not a measurement.

**Flip risk: MODERATE.** The undershoot is −2.42pp against a 3.61pp cost bar, so
arithmetic probably does carry it. But "probably" is doing the work in the one
place the thread's conclusion is symmetric.

### D2 · The crypto market-making verdict was never actually reached, and STATUS.md presents crypto as closed
**Verdict at risk:** *"Crypto ladder modelling — thread CLOSED."*
**Ledger status:** C022 **SETTLED (null)**; C025 **UNVERIFIED**.

Three things do not line up:

1. `crypto/MM_RESULTS.md` §10 is titled **"Verdict"** and its first two words are
   **"Not yet reached."** It states the decisive measurement — adverse selection
   on real `KXBTCD` flow at 373 ms — **has not been run**, and that gross margin
   at the touch is a full **1.00¢** with fees no longer foreclosing it.
2. **C025** — the circulating claim *"MM scan: 0 of 4 series profitable"* — has
   an artifact for **one** series (58 markets, `KXBTCD`). `EFFECTIVE_N_AUDIT.md`
   flags it `UNSUPPORTED`. Three of the four series were never P&L-tested.
3. `STATUS.md` lists the crypto thread as **CLOSED** on C010 (no model beats the
   mid), which is a *directional-forecast* result and says nothing about
   market-making.

**Flip risk: HIGH — and it is the only open positive lead in crypto.** The
project's own synthetic control is validated (BENIGN/NULL/TOXIC ordering passes,
the NULL arm lands at +0.0037¢ against a theoretical exact zero), so the
instrument to answer it exists and is trustworthy. The author's stated prior is
that it fails at 373 ms. That prior has not been tested.

### D3 · `kalshi-tennis` Stage 5 still contains a live overfitting hazard
**Verdict at risk:** any future re-run of Stage 5.
**Ledger status:** T021 **BROKEN**, *"still live in the code"*; T022 **BROKEN**.

`stage5_selective.py:255` sorts variants on `mean_pnl` **over the full sample
with no holdout**. `:99` dedupes with `keep="first"` — order-dependent and
non-deterministic. Stage 5's headline (T008, +14.4% to +24.6% ROI) is already
retracted for a *different* reason (mid-pricing), so nothing currently published
depends on these — **but the defects are in the code, not in a report**, and
anyone re-running Stage 5 inherits them silently.

### D4 · The weather thread cleared two of three gates and the third gate was never measured
**Verdict at risk:** *"Weather — worth the wait."*
**Ledger status:** K002 **SETTLED**, K004 **SETTLED**.

`KXTEMPDCH` is **the only family in the entire programme that clears both the
power bar and the capacity bar** (512 settlements against the 481 needed — a 6%
margin). The model genuinely beats climatology in all four cities with clustered
CIs excluding zero. And `GO_NO_GO.md` says of the deciding gate:

> **"Edge vs the mid: still unmeasured."**

**Flip risk: HIGH in both directions.** This is the largest genuinely-unexplored
lead in the repo. It is also the one place where the programme's standard
failure mode (a real effect smaller than the cost of reaching it) has not yet
been checked — and the 6% margin on recurrence means one recount could kill it.

### D5 · Four projects have ZERO rows in LEDGER.md
**Verdict at risk:** unknown, which is the point.

| project | results documents with no ledger rows |
|---|---|
| **bot-hunt** | `RESULTS.md`, `RESULTS_H10.md`, `RESULTS_CROSSVENUE.md`, `RESULTS_DEVIG.md` — **four** |
| **market-selection** | `SHORTLIST.md`, `killed.md`, and a `LEDGER_ADDITIONS.md` that nothing ever merged |
| **soccer** | `dataset.md`, `inplay_events.md` |
| **polymarket-tennis-copy** / **ptis-polymarket** | `docs/FINDINGS.md`, `outputs/*_REPORT.md` |

Both times this was done before, it immediately found a defect no one inside the
project could see. `bot-hunt` is the highest priority — it is the only project
still actively producing results, and it already carries at least one known
cross-project tension (its `RESULTS.md` MLB spread figure, corrected today).

### D6 · The soccer selection canary returned UNTESTABLE and was never closed
**Verdict at risk:** everything soccer.

`soccer/WHAT_IS_LEFT.md`, in its own words:

> *"Until this is done we do not know whether the 33% of matches carrying a
> closing line differ systematically from the 67% that do not."*

The fix is described as **~30 minutes** of work (use ESPN's independent final
score as the "without" arm). GUARDS #21 is explicit that **UNTESTABLE is a
verdict about the test, never about the effect** — so this is not a null, it is
an unknown sitting under a dataset.

### D7 · `kalshi-market-scan` K010 is load-bearing and OVERSTATED
**Verdict at risk:** the copy-trading kill (partly).

K010 — *"Kalshi pre-match prices are calibrated bucket by bucket"* — is a
**failure to reject**, not a demonstration: bucket CIs of ±11–29pp, 0 of 7
Polymarket values formally excluded. The ledger already re-labels it
UNVERIFIED/OVERSTATED, and K009's aggregate (−0.67pp on 762 settled matches)
carries the real weight.

**Mitigating, and it is strong:** **B027** independently confirmed the direction
on tennis by a different method — 0 of 10 price bands deviate on tradeable
books, pooled residual +0.03pp. So the *conclusion* now stands on two legs. The
defect is that the overstated wording is still live in `kalshi-market-scan`'s
own documents.

### D8 · The Kalshi retention deadline is contradicted by its own two measurements
**Verdict at risk:** any plan that assumes data disappears on 2026-08-19.

`market-selection/WHAT_IS_LEFT.md` calls this **"THE DECAYING ITEM"** — a 69-day
window rolling one day per day, everything gone by 2026-08-19. `bot-hunt`
bisected the same boundary on 08-04 and got the **same date, 2026-05-25** — i.e.
the window **grew** to 71 days rather than rolling. Two points is not enough to
overturn it; it is enough to stop treating the deadline as established.

**Flip risk: operational rather than statistical**, but expensive either way —
one direction wastes effort, the other loses irrecoverable history.

## 5. The question this audit raises that it cannot answer

Every closed thread died the same way: **a real effect smaller than the cost of
reaching it.** Tennis undershoot −2.42pp against a 3.61pp bar. Copy trading
+0.937pp against a ≥1.0pp spread. Player features +1.18pp on tradeable books
against a 5.15pp minimum detectable effect. De-vig on MLB: a 2.75¢ cost bar
against a **2.01pp total vig**.

**Four of the ten ranked items above are the same shape: a gate that was never
measured, on the one candidate whose cost bar might be small enough.** Weather
is the clearest — a model that genuinely beats climatology, on the only family
that clears both power and capacity, with the deciding comparison never run.

**That is the decision to make, and it is yours: is the next session spent
measuring weather's edge against the mid, or closing defects?** They are not
the same job and the repo has enough evidence to say which is bigger.
